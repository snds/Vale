# ADR-0106 · The note: its top line, title, chips, checklist and small layers

- **Status:** Accepted. It applies DC3 as [ADR-0105](0105-note-lists.md) proposed it (a note's color no longer tints the pane); the question for Sean (Q6) stands there.
- **Date:** 2026-11-14
- **Builds on:** [ADR-0028](0028-tasks-on-the-calendar.md) (checklists), [ADR-0034](0034-connected-notes.md) (`[[links]]`), [ADR-0035](0035-links-that-keep-up.md) (links to events, people and mail, renames), [ADR-0039](0039-note-reminders.md) (reminders), [ADR-0043](0043-locked-notes.md) (locks), [ADR-0056](0056-shared-notes.md) (shared notes), [ADR-0061](0061-presence-and-shared-files.md) (who is here), [ADR-0083](0083-shell.md) (the pushed screen's chrome), [ADR-0085](0085-search-place.md) (the row adapters), [ADR-0087](0087-forms-and-questions.md) (the form layer), [ADR-0088](0088-date-parts.md) (the date and time picker, the swatches), [ADR-0089](0089-writing-parts.md) (the format bar), [ADR-0105](0105-note-lists.md) (the swatch picker, DC3).
- **Design:** Mobile / 15 Notes — Note (`34:1360`) and Desktop / 11's detail column (`36:2571`); brief N2 of the undrawn-areas design, with 1.3, 1.4, 3.2 and 4.0; cross-app decisions §1, §9, §10, §12 and §13.

The open note was a tinted pane with a small grey "Edited Thu, Nov 12, 7:16 AM" line, a row of round icon buttons, pill-shaped chips, a first line set a little larger than the rest, native checkboxes, and a rename question that was a toast gone after 12 seconds. Each moves onto unicron's parts, as the frames draw it.

## Decisions

### The top line

- **The overline, as drawn:** `NOTE // EDITED 08:40` (the row's clock: today's time, a weekday within a week, then the date), then `// SHARED` for a note you share, `// SHARED // YOU CAN EDIT` or `// YOU CAN READ` for one shared with you, `// LOCKED` for a locked one, and `// ARCHIVED` in the Archive.
- **On a desktop, at the line's end:** who else has a shared note open (an Initial Tile S each, up to three, the names said to screen readers; `PeopleHere` gains an optional `tiles`, the team's views keep their faces), then unicron's `Toolbar` "Note": **Pin** (a toggle), **Remind**, **Color**, **More** (named "Note actions"). The toolbar is one Tab stop and stands across the overline's 16 px line, so the title sits 4 px under the words, as drawn.
- **More** (an `AdaptiveMenu`): Archive or Unarchive · Share… · Keep as my own note (a guest) · Copy as Markdown · Show on the map · Lock note, or Lock now and Remove lock · Delete. A shared note can't be locked and a locked one can't be shared, so only the row that applies shows. Lock now was a separate icon button; it is in More now. Show on the map turns the map on with the note picked; on a phone the note closes so the map shows.

### The title is a field of its own

- **The title is still the note's first line**, as every list, search, link and export reads it; the stored document is unchanged (the same Yjs document, the same Markdown). On screen it is a field above the chips, at the drawn size (`type-display-compact`, 48, on a desktop and at medium width; `type-title`, 32, on a phone), placeholder "Title", named "Title"; the text below is a second field, named "Text".
- The field edits the first block through the editor, writing only the characters that changed, so a title typed here syncs and merges as typing in the text does. The block itself is hidden in the text while it is the title (the first block, when it is a line of text: a paragraph or a heading with no image). A guard in the editor keeps every edit made in the text out of it, keeps the cursor below it, and sends ↑ on the first line and Backspace at the start up to the title field; Select all selects the text. A `[[link]]` in a title keeps its brackets in the field and becomes a link again.
- Enter or ↓ at the end of the title goes to the text, making a line when the note has only a title. A new note opens with the cursor in the title.

### The chips, LINKED FROM

- **One row:** the reminder (the time glyph and its time, or `Due ·` and its time once passed, and DONE), each link to an event, person or conversation (a press opens it; its × unlinks), each tag (its × removes it), then the text buttons **TAG** and **LINK**. The row always holds TAG and LINK, since they are the way to add the rest.
- TAG opens a small layer (a popover, a sheet on a phone) with one field that offers the tags in use, and ADD TAG. The inline round input is gone.
- **LINKED FROM:** the mono label and the notes that link here as chips, under the row, when there are any.

### The text

- **The body role (16/24)**, 12 px between blocks; headings in the lead and subtitle roles; lists, quotes (a 2 px rule, secondary ink), code (mono, on the hover wash), links in the identity ink, underlined.
- **Checklists as Mobile / 15:** each item is unicron's `Checkbox` (square, the ink fill and check when done) beside its words, 52 px a row with a 24 px line, and a hairline under each row; done words are struck through in the label ink. The box is named by the item's words. The row is a React node view of the same `taskItem`; the stored document and the Markdown are unchanged, and moving checked items down (ADR-0028) still applies.
- **`[[Title]]` links** are the identity ink, underlined; a link to a note that does not exist yet is the label ink (a decoration from the titles there are, archived ones included). Following it still starts that note.
- **The `[[` list** is drawn as unicron draws a menu: solid, square, a hairline round it, no shadow.

### The format bar

- f8's bar, with Heading and Checklist and the note's four actions (Link to a note, Add image, Scan a document, Sketch), unchanged. **On a desktop it is docked at the foot of the column; on a phone it shows only while you write** (the title, the text or the bar has focus), in the bottom row's place, above the keyboard. It stays mounted while hidden, so a layer it opened (Link, Sketch) stays open.
- No format bar on a note shared with you for reading.

### On a phone (Mobile / 15)

- The top bar: back, then **More** (a float button "Note actions") opening one sheet: Pin or Unpin, Remind me…, Color…, then More's rows. Remind and Color open their own sheet in its place.
- **The drawn add button** (the detail bottom row's primary, "Add line") continues the note with a line of the kind it ends with: a checklist item, a list item, or a paragraph, and puts the cursor there. An empty item or line already at the end is used as it is. While you write, the format bar takes the bottom row's place; on a note shared for reading there is no add button.

### Small layers

| Layer | Content |
|---|---|
| Remind | a menu headed REMIND ME (a sheet on a phone): the presets with their times · Pick a date and time… (the shared picker, SET REMINDER) · Remove reminder when one is set. Toasts "Reminder set for {time}", "Reminder removed" |
| Color | a popover titled Color (a sheet on a phone) holding the swatch picker: No color, then the ten colors as their 8 px square and name (`SwatchChoice kind="note"`). A pick closes it. The round tinted color buttons and the note's tint are gone (DC3) |
| Link this note | an `AdaptiveDialog`: a dialog of the form size (640) on a desktop, the whole screen on a phone. Overline NOTE, a one-line description, a `SearchField` "Search events, people and mail", then EVENTS, PEOPLE and CONVERSATIONS as `SectionHeader`s with counts over ADR-0085's adapters (`OccurrenceCard`, `PersonRow`, `MessageRow`). Empty search: what is nearest in time. A linked row carries LINKED; pressing it unlinks. No match: `LINK // 00` "Nothing matches "…"" |
| After a rename | a compact info `Banner` at the top of the note: "{N} notes link to "{old}"." with UPDATE LINKS and dismiss. It does not time out, and it stays when the note is closed and opened again in the session (`useRenameQuestions` in `rename-links.ts`). UPDATE LINKS points the links at the title the note has then; a failure turns the banner critical with the core's words |

### States

- **A new note left empty is thrown away on close** (as before).
- **Shared with you for reading:** a compact info Banner "Shared with you for reading." with "{owner} can change it. Their changes show up here."; the title and text can't be changed, the boxes can't be checked, no format bar, no add button.
- **Nothing open:** NOTES · "Nothing open." · "Pick a note to see it here." (MasterDetail, unchanged).
- **Gone:** NOTES · "That note is gone." · "It was deleted, or it was never on this device." **A failed read:** the critical empty state with the core's words and TRY AGAIN.
- **Loading:** two skeleton rows (a title bar and three lines).
- **Toasts** name the note, with no full stop: "Archived {title}", "Unarchived {title}", "Deleted {title}", each 6 s with UNDO; "Locked {title}", "Removed the lock from {title}", "{title} is your own note now", "Linked to {name}", "Unlinked {name}", "Updated links in N notes".

### Keys and screen readers

- **Esc leaves the text (or the title); Esc again closes the note.** The first Esc moves focus to the note itself (an `article` "Note"); a second, from anywhere in the note but a field, closes it. Esc inside a layer closes the layer only.
- The title and the text are two named fields; checklist items are checkboxes; the note's toolbar and the format bar are one Tab stop each.

## Verification

- `tsc -b`, `oxlint` (no warnings), `vite build` (the app and the demo) and the notes relay's tests pass.
- Ratchet, counted by reading the source: className overrides **106 → 92**, style overrides **1 → 0** (the color menu's tinted buttons), spread 0, plain buttons 12. `.dsa/baseline.json` is lowered. Look-alikes by the estimator: 0.
- In the browser, at phone (390 × 844), medium (768 × 1024) and desktop (1440 × 900), stone and graphite, by asserting scenes: the overline, the title as its own field at 32 and 48 px, four checkboxes with the done one struck through in another ink and a hairline under each row, no tint, the link chip; the desktop toolbar (Pin, Remind me, Color: Green, Note actions; one Tab stop) and docked bar; the phone's More, the add button and no bar until you write. A checklist item checks and is still checked when the note is read again from the core (the demo is in memory, so this is a close and reopen, not a page reload), and unchecks. Esc leaves the text and a second Esc closes the note; Esc in the title only leaves it. Pin turns off (the PINNED section goes) and on; a preset reminder shows its chip and DONE takes it away; the color goes to blue and back; a tag goes on and off; a person is linked from the form, carries LINKED there, and a press unlinks; archive and delete each raise their toast and UNDO brings the note back; the link form is 640 wide on a desktop and the whole screen on a phone, with LINKED on the linked event; "Nothing matches"; the add button adds a fifth checklist item with the cursor in it and the bar up; a rename raises the banner, the other note's link turns to the label ink, and the banner is there again after reopening; UPDATE LINKS points the link at the new title; a note shared for reading has the banner, no bar, a read-only title and text; the color layer holds No color and ten colors with Green picked; the remind layer holds the presets and Pick a date and time…, and no Remove reminder without a reminder. The shell's and search's scenes that open a note still pass.

## Consequences

- The note looks like Mobile / 15 and Desktop / 11, and the layers around it use the same parts as the rest of Vale.
- The title field is a view over the first line, not a stored field of its own: nothing about notes changes on disk or on other devices. A note whose first block is a list or an image has no title block; its field is empty and typing there adds one.
- Unicron gaps found here: the row adapters have no slot for a status tag (LINKED stands in a column beside the row); a layer opened from another layer's row has no trigger of its own (a hidden button stands in, and focus falls back to the page); `ToolbarToggle` draws "on" in inverted ink, so the note's Pin and the bar's Checklist are the heaviest marks on a resting note.
