# ADR-0080 · unicron 0.24: current again, before the redesign

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Builds on:** [ADR-0074](0074-unicron-0-16.md) (unicron 0.16), [ADR-0079](0079-screenshots.md) (screenshots of every screen)

unicron supports its current minor and the one before it. Vale was eight behind, on `@unicron/ui` 0.16.0 and `@unicron/tokens` 0.10.0. The redesign will move to a newer release again, and that step should carry only the redesign. This moves Vale to 0.24.0 and 0.16.0, the release Warpcore is on.

## Decisions

- **The two URLs** (`apps/web/package.json`, and the tokens override in `pnpm-workspace.yaml`). ui 0.17 brings one new dependency, `react-resizable-panels` 4.14.8.
- **Three dialogs drop their padding by the token, not a class:** the editor frame, the composer and the booking page preview go from `p-0` to `[--c-dialog-padding:0]`. Since ui 0.17 `DialogContent` pads by `--c-dialog-padding` and a footer reaches out by the same amount, so a `p-*` class would leave a footer short of the edges. None of the three has a footer today, and nothing on screen moves.
- **A spinner inside a status that already speaks is hidden from screen readers.** Since ui 0.23 the `Spinner` says "Loading" itself. The sign-in wait (`oauth-sign-in.tsx`) and the AI model check (`ai-settings.tsx`) already say what's happening, so they would be read twice. The other two (the image viewer, the attachment preview) stand alone, and now say "Loading" where they said nothing.
- **Two phone selects make room for 16 px text.** Since ui 0.18, `NativeSelect` and the ⌘K search field keep 16 px text on touch screens so iOS doesn't zoom into them (14 px with a mouse, as before). Two selects then cut their words short on a phone: the swipe choices in Shortcuts and gestures ("Same as a short swi…") no longer shrink below their width (`shrink-0`), and the event editor's time zone ("Yours · Los Angeles (P…") may take 70% of its row on a touch screen (60% with a mouse, as before).
- **Take the rest as it is.** What 0.17 to 0.24 change in Vale:
  - On a phone, every select and the ⌘K search field read at 16 px; inputs and text areas already did. Nothing else moves, and the desktop is unchanged.
  - `animate-spin` and `animate-pulse` run on the motion roles, with a reduced-motion layer for everything (the sync icon, skeletons, spinners).
  - Side and bottom sheets keep clear of the notch, and `Empty` and `Textarea` tighten up on a short (landscape phone) screen. That's from unicron's notes: the pictures have no notch and no landscape.
  - New status roles (`success`, `attention`, `running`): Vale defines none of these names, so nothing clashes.
- **Not adopted here:** `UpdateStatusRow` and `AutoCheckSwitch` (0.22) in place of Vale's About section, `Popover`, `Select`, `Progress`, the `indicator` prop, and `AppFrame` (0.18 to 0.21). The redesign decides which it wants; `AppFrameNav`'s floating glass bar is the opposite of the new direction.

## Verification

- `tsc -b`, `oxlint`, `vite build`, `pnpm build:demo` and the notes relay tests are clean.
- **Ratchet:** flat at 208 className overrides, 2 style overrides, 0 spread props and 16 raw elements, read from the source (the edits change existing classes and add `aria-hidden`; `dsa` itself couldn't be fetched where this was written, so CI runs it).
- **Screenshots** (`pnpm shots`, 296 pictures, before and after): every desktop picture is the same within noise (at most 39 pixels, a shade apart). 62 phone pictures differ, 31 scenes in both themes, and each shows only selects and the ⌘K field at 16 px, with the two fixed selects whole again.
- The lockfile changes only the two unicron entries and adds `react-resizable-panels`; the `@snds/dsa` entries are unchanged.

## Consequences

- **Selects no longer make iOS zoom in.** A select with a fixed width may need room for 16 px text on a phone; the two that cut words short are fixed here.
- **Screen readers hear "Loading"** on a spinner that stands alone.
