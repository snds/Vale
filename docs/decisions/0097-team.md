# ADR-0097 · Working mail with other people: the Team lane, a shared conversation, the shared inbox

- **Status:** Accepted for the build. It records no recorded decision as changed. It keeps ADR-0060's sharing by link, ADR-0061's presence and shared files, ADR-0062's sealed links and ADR-0063's shared inbox as they are, and only redraws them.
- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Builds on:** [ADR-0060](0060-team-conversations.md) (conversations shared by link), [ADR-0061](0061-presence-and-shared-files.md) (who is here, shared files), [ADR-0062](0062-end-to-end-encrypted-sync.md) (sealed links), [ADR-0063](0063-shared-inbox.md) (the shared inbox), [ADR-0086](0086-status.md) (`LoadState`, `useCopy`), [ADR-0087](0087-forms-and-questions.md) (the form layer, `useConfirm`), [ADR-0089](0089-writing-parts.md) (the rich-text field), [ADR-0092](0092-conversation.md) (the conversation and its team slot).
- **Design:** brief M9 of the undrawn-areas design; contract 5.1b #58 (`Comment`, app-level in Vale) and #30 (`FileChip`); cross-app decisions §1, §9, §10, §12, §13.

ADR-0060 drew a shared conversation as one long bordered panel and the share as a plain dialog. This redraws both on unicron's parts, splits the panel into tabs, gives the Team lane its own head, and puts the owner's and the shared inbox's team side under one header after the messages. Every action in `features/team` stays, as do the privacy lines and the owner's right to delete any team note.

## Decisions

### Share with teammates (brief M9 A)

1. **The form layer** (`FormLayer`, form size; the whole screen on a phone), overline the conversation's subject, title "Share with teammates". Three states:
   - **No notes relay:** "Sharing goes through your notes relay, the same one that syncs your notes." and a secondary OPEN SYNC, which closes the form and calls `openSyncSetup()`.
   - **Not shared:** "People with the link read it, comment, and help write the reply. Only you send it." and the primary CREATE LINK. A refusal stays in the form as its critical banner, in the server's words.
   - **Shared:** a Field Row LINK with the link cut in the middle and COPY LINK, which says "Copied" for 2 s and raises no toast (`useCopy`); a secondary EMAIL IT (a new message to a teammate, as before); a text STOP SHARING that asks "Stop sharing?" / "The link stops working." / STOP SHARING with focus on Cancel (`useConfirm`), then says "Stopped sharing {subject}".
2. **Captions:** "Teammates get the text, not attachments or images." always, and "Your relay can read it, as it can read your notes." when the share has no key (a sealed relay makes sealed links, ADR-0062).
3. On a phone the form's top row holds the one action as its float button (f6's form layer always draws it there): OPEN SYNC, CREATE LINK or EMAIL IT.

### The Team lane (brief M9 A)

1. **Page head:** overline `TEAM // NN`, the number of shared conversations both ways, and no account (what is shared is not per account); title "Team"; head action OPEN A LINK… (a text button).
2. **Rows:** `ListRow layout="inline"`: the title, "You shared" or "From {name}" as plain meta, a Badge STOPPED once the owner stopped, and the time it last changed (`updated`) as the frames write row times. The selected row is primary-soft with the edge. The lane no longer imports `components/list-row.tsx`.
3. **Empty** (brief 5.2): `TEAM // 00`, "Nothing shared.", "Share a conversation from its menu.", OPEN A LINK…. Loading: skeleton rows. A failed read: `LoadState`.
4. **OPEN A LINK…** is a form (overline Team, title "Open a shared conversation"): a Text Field LINK and the primary OPEN, off until the text is a conversation link. A note's link says where it opens instead.

### A shared conversation (brief M9 A)

1. **MetaList:** SHARED BY ("You", or the teammate's name) · STATE, a Badge: LIVE in the live tone while the relay is connected, CONNECTING, or STOPPED · HERE, an Initial Tile S per person who has it open, named, shown only when someone is.
2. **The title**, then on a desktop a Toolbar beside the MetaList: Sharing… (the owner, while shared) and Leave (a guest), or Remove once stopped. On a phone it is a pushed screen whose top bar holds Back and More with the same actions, and its bottom row has no Composer.
3. **Line tabs:** MESSAGES · COMMENTS NN · REPLY · FILES. The tab list scrolls sideways on a phone (a plain wrapper). Each tab's field and button come after its content.
   - **MESSAGES:** the shared copy, oldest first, every message open, read-only, on rules with no cards. No Composer.
   - **COMMENTS:** `CommentRow`s (`features/team/comment.tsx`, #58's anatomy from core parts: Initial Tile S, the name, the time, the text, DELETE on your own); then a multi-line Text Field "Comment for the team…" with a secondary ADD COMMENT (⌘ or Ctrl + Return too); the caption "Comments stay with the team."
   - **REPLY:** the shared rich-text field "Write the reply together…" on f8's `RichEditor` and `FormatBar`. The owner's secondary USE AS REPLY opens the composer with it, as a reply on their real conversation. A guest reads who sends it.
   - **FILES:** `FileChip`s. The owner's lists every attachment with SHARE or STOP SHARING and "Shared" or "Only you" after the size; over 10 MB the chip's critical state says "Over 10 MB, too big to share" and offers nothing. A guest's has DOWNLOAD.
4. **States:** stopped for a guest: a compact info Banner "The owner stopped sharing." (with "What you had stays here as your copy. Only you can see it.") and everything read-only. Removed from the owner's mail: a compact info Banner "That conversation isn't in your mail any more." with REMOVE, which asks first while the link still works ("The link stops working, and the comments and the reply go with it."). Toasts name the thing: "Left {subject}", "Removed {subject}".

### The owner's copy and the shared inbox (brief M9 A and B)

1. **One team block** in the conversation's team slot, after the messages: `SectionHeader` TEAM // NOT SENT, then the shared inbox's side, then (while shared by link) COMMENTS, REPLY and FILES as tabs. The pinned reply field stays the screen's only Composer.
2. **The shared inbox:** a Field Row ASSIGNED TO with the name ("{name} (you)" for you) or "Nobody", who assigned it as its description, and ASSIGN, an `AdaptiveMenu` radio list of the other members, Me and Nobody. Then the team's notes as `CommentRow`s (the gateway's owner may delete anyone's; a member, their own); a multi-line Text Field "Note for the team…" with a secondary ADD NOTE; the caption "Notes stay with the team. They never go to anyone on the conversation." A failed change says so beside its control in the server's words.
3. **HERE** joins the conversation's MetaList when anyone else has it open: the shared inbox's team (from the gateway) and teammates on a shared copy (from the relay).
4. **A team member is on a shared inbox** (`useSharedInbox`): they see Assigned to me and the team block even before the members list loads. On a real gateway the list already has two people, so this changes nothing there; in the demo it lets the `member` scenario show it.
5. **Team fields are plain text fields with their own button**, never the reply field, so a note or a comment can never go out as mail.

### Smaller

- `useTeamDoc` asks for its document again once shared conversations start syncing (`useTeamSyncStarts`): a conversation opened before the sync started (at start-up, or as the relay changed) showed its skeleton for good.
- Demo scenarios in `core/demo-scenarios/mail.ts`: `team-shared` (a relay, your share of Token audit with two attachments, one over 10 MB, a teammate's share, a stopped one), `team-removed` (with it: the conversation gone from your mail) and `shared-inbox` (with `member`: Token audit assigned to the member, two notes, and Assigned to me filtered as the gateway filters it).

## Verification

- `check-mail-search.sh`: typecheck, lint, build:demo, build, the notes-relay tests, the ratchet by reading and the lockfile proof, all green.
- **Ratchet:** className **82 → 82**, style 1, spread 0, raw buttons 14. The team files had no className on a unicron part to take away, and this adds none; the look-alike estimate stays 0 (`CommentRow` is not a unicron name).
- **Scenes** (`tools/screenshots/scenes/mail.mjs`), phone and desktop, graphite and stone, 48 pictures: `mail-team`, `team-empty`, `mail-team-open-link`, `mail-share`, `mail-share-create`, `mail-share-link`, `team-shared`, `team-owner-copy`, `team-stopped`, `team-removed`, `team-assigned`. They assert the lane's rows and overline, the empty state, the form states and their words, STOP SHARING's question with focus on Cancel, the four tabs and their counts, adding a comment, delete only on your own, no Composer on a shared conversation and exactly one on the owner's copy and the assigned conversation, the 10 MB chip, USE AS REPLY reaching the composer, the stopped and removed banners, ASSIGN's Me and Nobody, and a member's delete only on their own note.
- **A local notes relay** (`tools/notes-relay`, a fresh data folder): the owner created a link; a guest in a second browser context opened it from Team and saw LIVE and the three messages; the guest's comment and the reply typed together reached the owner's copy; HERE showed the guest; USE AS REPLY opened the composer with the team's reply; STOP SHARING turned the guest's copy read-only with "The owner stopped sharing."
- **Not verified:** the shared inbox on a real gateway (the debug gateway was not built); the demo shows it through the `member` and `shared-inbox` scenarios.

## Consequences

- `features/team/team-panel.tsx` is now `TeamTabs`; `CommentList` became `CommentThread` and `CommentRow` in `comment.tsx`. Nothing outside `features/team` and Mail used them.
- `ShareThreadLayer` and `OpenTeamLinkLayer` (named `…Dialog` until the merge, renamed because a name that ends in a part it does not render reads as a look-alike) keep their props, so Mail's conversation and the Team lane open them as before.
- When unicron ships `Comment` (Warpcore tracker w3-04), `CommentRow` is replaced by it in this one file.
